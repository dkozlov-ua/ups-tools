Fixes the time left on battery, which the Elite 100 V2 showed about six times too long (e.g. 41 h instead of
about 7 h).

- The Elite 100 V2 reports its time left in minutes; UPS Tools read it as tenths of an hour. The EL10 and EL30V2
  are read in minutes too now.
- Even read correctly, the UPS's own estimate tends to be longer than its battery can last at the current load. The
  time left is now the lower of the UPS's figure and the stored energy divided by the current load, so *Battery low*
  doesn't fire late. When the calculated figure is used, the dashboard shows the UPS's own figure next to it.
- `UPS_RUNTIME_MINUTES` and the notifications use the same, corrected figure.

Existing settings and scripts carry over as they are.
