The time left on battery is now worked out by UPS Tools itself, from the average load.

- **Time left** is the energy stored in the battery divided by the average load of the last 5 minutes. A short
  change in load no longer makes it jump.
- The UPS's own estimate isn't used any more. On the Elite 100 V2 it was much longer than the battery can last at
  the current load and changed sharply with it (0.4.1 used whichever of the two was lower).
- *Battery low*, the notifications and `UPS_RUNTIME_MINUTES` all use the new figure. The dashboard shows the average
  load it is based on.
- Right after UPS Tools starts or reconnects, the average covers only the readings so far.

Existing settings and scripts carry over as they are.
