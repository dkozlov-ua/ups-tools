- **UPS not found after another PC had it**: a UPS connected to another PC (or phone) stops announcing itself, and
  Windows forgets it; once it was free again, UPS Tools kept failing with "Bluetooth device not found" until the UPS
  was removed and added again. It now listens for the UPS for a few seconds first, as adding it does, and connects.
- **Network sharing**: a PC that let another PC try a UPS it couldn't reach tries again at once when that PC goes
  away (e.g. into standby), instead of waiting 5 minutes.
