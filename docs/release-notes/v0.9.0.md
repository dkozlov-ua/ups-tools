With several UPSes, the dashboard now shows all of them at once, and each UPS can be left out of the tray icon's
tooltip and colour.

- **Every UPS on the dashboard**: with more than one paired, each has a compact panel with all its readings: grid,
  battery, charge rate, time left, input and load, in a row of six tiles (two rows of three in a narrow window). A
  detail that doesn't fit shows in full when you point at it; past about two thirds of the window's height, the
  panels scroll. The UPS picker next to the tabs now only chooses what Scripts and Configuration show. With one UPS,
  the dashboard is as before.
- **Battery low turns the tray icon red** until the grid is back, and that UPS's tooltip line says *Battery low*.
- **The tray icon, per UPS**: Configuration → Tray icon has
  - **Show in the tooltip**: leave a UPS out of the tooltip, e.g. when Windows cuts the lines of several UPSes short.
  - **Grid lost**, **Battery low**, **UPS unreachable**: whether these events change the icon's colour. With one off,
    that UPS leaves the icon alone: on battery, it counts as on the grid; with its battery low, as on battery;
    unreachable, as its last reading says. Its notifications and scripts don't change.

  Every UPS starts with all of them on.
- **"UPS Tools is still running"** shows the first time you close the window to the tray, and not again: before, it
  came back after every update, restart or sign-in.

Nothing changes for your scripts: the same events fire at the same times.
