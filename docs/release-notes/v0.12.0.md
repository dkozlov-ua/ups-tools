With network sharing on, a PC or phone on mains power takes over the UPS from one running on its own battery.

- **Mains power first**: for each UPS, the device connected to it over Bluetooth is now one on mains power when there
  is one, so a laptop or a phone on its battery doesn't drain it holding the link. When a laptop connected to the UPS
  is unplugged, or a PC on mains power joins, that PC takes the UPS over within seconds; when they start together,
  the one on mains power connects. A desktop, a laptop plugged in (also into the UPS during an outage) and a phone on
  a charger, a PC's USB port or a wireless pad count as on mains power.
  - Being plugged in counts after a minute, so a quick charge moves nothing. Two devices on mains power, or two on
    battery, don't take the UPS from each other.
  - One on mains power that can't reach the UPS once it took over (e.g. out of Bluetooth range) hands it back after
    2 minutes, as before, and doesn't take it over again for an hour.
- **Sharing card**: the other devices on battery are marked *(on battery)*.

This changes which device holds the Bluetooth connection, not what the others see: while the UPS changes hands its
readings just wait, so no *UPS unreachable* fires, and your scripts run as before. Devices of 0.11.0 and earlier still
share with this version; while one of them has the UPS paired, the fixed order decides who connects, as before. Update every
PC and phone to get the new behaviour.
