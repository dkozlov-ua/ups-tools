A History chart on Windows and on the phone; on the phone, webhooks, a Quick Settings tile and home-screen widgets.

- **History** (Windows and Android): a chart of each UPS's charge and load over the last 6 hours, 24 hours or 7 days,
  with the outages shaded, the stretches without readings greyed and the events marked; pointing at it (touching it,
  on the phone) shows the reading there. Each device keeps a point a minute for 7 days, also of a UPS it reads
  through another one, so the chart fills from the moment you update.
  - On Windows, it's the new **History** tab, for the UPS picked at the top right.
  - On the phone, tap a UPS on the dashboard: its chart, with the events of that time, and its settings a button away.
- **Webhooks** (Android): each UPS's settings can have URLs its events call with a POST, as scripts are on Windows:
  JSON with the event and the UPS's state (the scripts' variables; for Home Assistant, n8n, Node-RED) or the
  notification's text with its title in a header (for ntfy). A webhook that fails is tried twice more, the log says
  how it went, and **Send test** tries one.
- **Tasker, MacroDroid, Automate** (Android): every event is also broadcast to the apps on the phone as
  `com.dkozlov.upstools.EVENT`, with the same fields as extras.
- **Quick Settings tile** (Android): the state (e.g. *On battery*) with the charge and load; tap it to open the app.
- **Widgets** (Android):
  - **UPS battery**: one UPS, chosen when you add it (add one for each UPS): its state in colour, its charge as a bar,
    and the load with the time left or the charging. Tapping it opens that UPS's history.
  - **All UPSes**, the widget there was: larger text, and it starts two rows tall (resize one you have, if its last
    line is cut off).

Nothing changes for your scripts on Windows, and PCs and phones of 0.10.0 and 0.11.0 share UPSes with each other as
before.
